Part 1: Multiple-Choice Questions (20)

Choose ONE correct answer from the four options.

Question 1. What is an open economy?

  • A. An economy that does not trade with other countries.
  • B. An economy that interacts with other countries through trade and financial flows.
  • C. An economy that produces only agricultural products.
  • D. An economy that prohibits foreign investment.
Correct answer: B. An economy that interacts with other countries through trade and financial flows.
Explanation: An open economy engages in international trade, imports, exports, foreign investment, and cross-border financial transactions.

Question 2. How do we calculate net exports (NX)?

  • A. Imports minus exports.
  • B. Consumption minus investment.
  • C. Exports minus imports.
  • D. Government spending minus taxes.
Correct answer: C. Exports minus imports.
Explanation: The formula is NX = X - M. If exports are $500 million and imports are $400 million, net exports are $100 million, a trade surplus.

Question 3. Which formula represents national income (GDP) in an open economy?

  • A. Y = C + I + G
  • B. Y = C + I + G + (X - M)
  • C. Y = C - I - G
  • D. Y = X + M - G
Correct answer: B. Y = C + I + G + (X - M)
Explanation: Open-economy GDP includes consumption, investment, government spending, and net exports. Imports are subtracted to remove foreign-produced goods and services.

Question 4. If 1 USD = 4,100 KHR, how many riels can you exchange for $100?

  • A. 4,100 KHR
  • B. 40,100 KHR
  • C. 400,000 KHR
  • D. 410,000 KHR
Correct answer: D. 410,000 KHR.
Explanation: 100 × 4,100 = 410,000 KHR.

Question 5. From Cambodia's perspective, what type of quotation is 1 USD = 4,100 KHR?

  • A. Direct quotation.
  • B. Indirect quotation.
  • C. Real exchange rate.
  • D. Purchasing power parity.
Correct answer: A. Direct quotation.
Explanation: A direct quotation expresses the price of one unit of foreign currency in domestic currency.

Question 6. The exchange rate falls from 4,100 KHR/USD to 4,000 KHR/USD. What happens to the riel against the dollar?

  • A. It depreciates.
  • B. It becomes worthless.
  • C. It appreciates.
  • D. It remains unchanged.
Correct answer: C. It appreciates.
Explanation: One US dollar now buys fewer riels, so the riel has strengthened against the dollar.

Question 7. What is the real exchange rate?

  • A. The rate determined only by the central bank.
  • B. The nominal exchange rate adjusted for relative price levels.
  • C. The rate used only by commercial banks.
  • D. The amount of money printed by a country.
Correct answer: B. The nominal exchange rate adjusted for relative price levels.
Explanation: The real exchange rate considers the nominal exchange rate and price levels, helping compare relative prices and competitiveness.

Question 8. What does the Nominal Effective Exchange Rate (NEER) measure?

  • A. A weighted average of nominal exchange rates against trading partners.
  • B. The price of one product in two countries.
  • C. Domestic inflation only.
  • D. Foreign currency held by commercial banks.
Correct answer: A. A weighted average of nominal exchange rates against trading partners.
Explanation: NEER summarizes movements against a basket of currencies, generally weighted by trade importance. REER also adjusts for relative prices or costs.

Question 9. If foreign consumers increase demand for Cambodian goods, what is likely to happen to demand for the riel?

  • A. Demand for KHR falls to zero.
  • B. Supply of KHR must disappear.
  • C. Demand for KHR shifts left.
  • D. Demand for KHR shifts right, putting upward pressure on its value.
Correct answer: D. Demand for KHR shifts right, putting upward pressure on its value.
Explanation: Foreign buyers may need riels to purchase Cambodian goods. Greater demand can cause appreciation, other things equal.

Question 10. What is a likely effect of domestic currency depreciation on trade?

  • A. Exports automatically become more expensive for foreign buyers.
  • B. Exports become cheaper for foreign buyers and imports more expensive for domestic buyers.
  • C. Exports and imports always disappear.
  • D. The trade balance must improve immediately.
Correct answer: B. Exports become cheaper for foreign buyers and imports more expensive for domestic buyers.
Explanation: Depreciation can improve competitiveness, but the trade balance may not improve immediately because quantities adjust over time.

Question 11. What is the main idea of the Law of One Price?

  • A. Every country must use the same currency.
  • B. All products must have the same production cost.
  • C. Identical tradable goods should have the same price in a common currency under ideal conditions.
  • D. Interest rates must be equal everywhere.
Correct answer: C. Identical tradable goods should have the same price in a common currency under ideal conditions.
Explanation: The idea assumes comparable goods, competitive markets, and no significant transportation costs or trade barriers.

  • A. The domestic currency tends to depreciate over time.
  • B. It must appreciate immediately.
  • C. The exchange rate can never change.
  • D. Inflation has no relationship with exchange rates.
